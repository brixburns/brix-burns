Deflationary Mechanics

To mint a TRIXSTER NFT, users must either burn $BRIX tokens or use an alternative SOL option.

$BRIX Burn Path

  • Tokens are permanently destroyed
  • Burned tokens never return to circulation
  • No portion of burned $BRIX goes to the team or treasury
  • Any undistributed rewards remain inside the ecosystem pool

Alternative SOL Burn Path

Users may also choose a 0.4 SOL alternative instead of burning $BRIX. In that case:

  • 50% is used for $BRIX buyback and burn
  • 50% goes into the reward pool

This creates ongoing pressure toward reducing circulating $BRIX supply regardless of the path chosen.

Mint Structure

The collection is divided into four progressive mint phases. Each phase follows the same cycle:

  • Burn Access
  • Mint
  • Hidden Pre-Reveal Period
  • Reveal
  • Jackpot Distribution
  • Reward Distribution

This structure creates an evolving ecosystem rather than a single static mint event.

Pre-Reveal

Before reveal, NFT rarity is completely hidden. No rarity traits are visible, and all NFTs display a temporary placeholder image. NFTs remain fully tradable on secondary markets during this period.

The goal is to maximize fairness, suspense, market speculation, and community engagement.

Reveal

At the end of each phase:

  • NFTs are revealed — final artwork becomes visible
  • Real rarity tiers are publicly assigned
  • Reward eligibility is unlocked

Each phase has its own reveal event. The hidden window creates a speculative secondary market before rarity is known — anyone holding during the pre-reveal period is playing with equal information.

The collection contains five rarity tiers. Higher rarity tiers receive larger reward allocations and increased jackpot weighting.

Unminted NFTs

NFTs that remain unminted by the end of a phase do not exist on-chain. They will be held in reserve for potential future uses, the details of which may be announced separately.

How the Pool Works

Every NFT mint contributes 0.25 SOL into a shared reward pool. The pool is funded by mint payments and part of the alternative SOL burn option. It is distributed to holders after each phase reveal, based on NFT rarity.

Perpetual Pool

Beyond the initial mint phases, the ecosystem is designed to support ongoing holder incentives. Secondary market royalties and ecosystem fees are reinvested into a perpetual pool distributed to long-term holders through periodic snapshots.

The system is designed to reward loyalty — holders who remain invested over time will receive preferential weighting in future distributions. The longer you hold, the more the ecosystem works in your favor.
